# Privacy Policy: Duplicate Tab Guard

Duplicate Tab Guard does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any user data. It accesses the URLs and titles of open tabs in memory solely to detect duplicate tabs, and shows them only to you, in the extension's own popup and welcome page, so you can choose which duplicates to close. Nothing is saved to disk, and nothing leaves your browser. It makes no network requests and contains no analytics or tracking.
